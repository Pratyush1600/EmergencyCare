🚑 EmergencyCare 360 – Smart Emergency Healthcare Platform

EmergencyCare 360 is a web-based healthcare platform designed to provide quick access to emergency medical assistance, essential healthcare information, and support during critical situations. Its goal is to make emergency care more accessible, efficient, and user-friendly through technology.

The platform connects patients and hospital staff through a centralized system for emergency registration, patient information management, status tracking, and hospital resource coordination.

🎯 Problem Statement

Hospitals often face challenges during emergency situations, including:

Delays caused by manual patient registration.

Scattered patient information and medical records.

Communication gaps between patients, families, and hospital staff.

Difficulty tracking patient status and movement.

Limited visibility into available hospital beds and resources.

EmergencyCare 360 aims to address these challenges through a unified digital platform.
